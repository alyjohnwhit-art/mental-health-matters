Evidence-based information at supporting documentation.

Project Summary

Ang Mental Health Matters ay isang comprehensive educational website na specially designed para i-promote ang mental health awareness at hikayatin ang resilience sa mga individuals at communities. Ang website ay nag-provide ng reliable, madaling maintindihang information tungkol sa mental well-being, common mental health challenges, healthy coping strategies, prevention methods, at available support resources.

Through six informative webpages, ang project ay nag-aim na mag-educate ng users, mag-reduce ng stigma surrounding mental health, at mag-encourage ng positive mental health practices. Ang website ay nag-emphasize ng importance ng early awareness, self-care, at seeking professional support when necessary.

Project Members

Alayan, Orea, De Vela, Bustamante

Project Objectives

General Objective

I-promote ang mental health awareness through providing reliable information at practical strategies na tumutulong sa individuals na mag-build ng resilience at mag-encourage ng supportive, mentally healthy communities.

Specific Objectives
  • Turuan ang users tungkol sa importance ng mental health at emotional well-being.
  • Mag-increase ng awareness ng common mental health challenges at warning signs.
  • Magbigay ng practical resilience-building techniques at coping strategies.
  • Hikayatin ang individuals na mag-seek ng professional support.
  • Mag-reduce ng stigma associated with mental health conditions.
  • Mag-promote ng positive mental health practices at self-care.

Research Findings & Evidence

Mental Health Statistics
  • 1 in 5 adults ay nag-eexperience ng mental illness annually.
  • 50% ng mental illness ay nagsisimula bago mag-age 14.
  • 75% ng people sa developing countries ay walang treatment.
  • $1 trillion annual economic cost ng mental health conditions.
  • Suicide ay kabilang sa leading causes of death.
  • Early intervention ay maaaring mag-prevent ng 70-80% ng severe cases.
Treatment Effectiveness
  • Therapy at counseling ay effective para sa 75% ng individuals.
  • CBT ay may humigit-kumulang 60% remission rate para sa depression.
  • Regular exercise ay nakababawas ng depression at anxiety.
  • Mindfulness at meditation ay nakababawas ng stress.
  • Strong social support ay protective factor.
  • Early treatment improves long-term outcomes.

Key Takeaways

Mental Health Matters

Ang mental health ay kasing-importante ng physical health at integral part ng overall well-being.

It's Treatable

Ang mental health conditions ay treatable at maraming tao ang nakakarecover with proper support.

Early Help Counts

Ang early intervention ay malaking tulong upang maiwasan ang paglala ng mental health conditions.

Support is Available

Maraming organizations, professionals, at support groups ang handang tumulong.

Build Resilience

Ang resilience ay skill na maaaring ma-develop through healthy coping strategies at self-care.

Community Matters

Ang supportive communities ay mahalagang bahagi ng mental health recovery at overall well-being.
